# Divide by Pizza (Team 19)

## Our Game:

### The Main Game

The player (YOU!) just inherited the family pizza shop and has to keep it 
running through a busy week. Customers will line up at the counter with orders 
written as fractions, such as “⅗ of the pizza with pepperoni please!” Each 
customer will have a patience meter that slowly runs down as they wait.

### Over Timer Minigame

During lunch rush, the player runs the ovens! Pizza bake times are given in 
fractions of a minute, but the oven dial is only marked in decimals from zero 
to one. The player turns the dial to the matching decimal: too low and the 
pizza comes out raw; too high and it burns! Early rounds use a dial marked in 
tenths.

### Stock Supply Minigame

Before the shop opens each morning, a delivery truck arrives with ingredient 
containers labeled in a mix of fractions and decimals, such as 1/2 lb, 0.3 
lb, 3/5 lb, etc. The player drags the containers onto a shelf in order from 
least to greatest before the truck driver gets tired of waiting. To compare 
them, the player has to convert the fractions to decimals in their head.
