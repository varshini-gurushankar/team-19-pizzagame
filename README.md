# Divide by Pizza (Team 19)

---

## Our Game:

The player (YOU!) just inherited the family pizza shop and has to keep it running through a busy week. Customers will line up at the counter with orders written as fractions, such as “⅗ of the pizza with pepperoni please!”. Each customer will have a patience meter that slowly runs down as they wait.

---

## Current Features:

We've mostly focused on the main-game aspect. Once you click the "Main Game" button on the home page, you see on the left-hand side the order details and patience meter for the customer. In the middle, you have the pizza with a slice-slider and toppings below. On the right, you have the serve button that has success/failure popups.
