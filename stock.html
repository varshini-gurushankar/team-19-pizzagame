MORNING DELIVERY MINI-GAME

STOCK SUPPLY

Sort smart. Stock fast. Open on time.

Before the shop opens, ingredient boxes arrive labeled with whole numbers, fractions, and decimals. Stack them from lightest to heaviest before the driver gets tired of waiting!

STOCK SUPPLY

HOW TO PLAY

  1. You get 8 boxes. Each one has a weight in pounds, like 2, 3/5, 2 1/3, or 0.4.
  2. Drag each box onto the shelf, or click a box and then click a slot. Slot 1 is the lightest and slot 8 is the heaviest.
  3. When every box is on the shelf, press Check order.
  4. Finish before the driver's patience bar runs out!

Tip: turn every weight into a decimal to compare them. For example, 3/5 = 0.6 and 2 1/3 ≈ 2.33.
